About

Barracuda in Antwerp is the perfect spot for a budget-friendly night out with friends. Known for its cozy, yet fashionably casual atmosphere, it offers a delightful mix of lively energy and comfortable surroundings. With an average rating of 4.3 stars on Google, visitors praise its friendly and awesome service. The pub serves a wide variety of alcohol, including cocktails, beer, and wine, all at affordable prices, typically ranging from 1 to 10 EUR per person. Enjoy delicious shots while taking advantage of the outdoor seating, perfect for socializing on pleasant evenings. Open until midnight, Barracuda is the ideal place to unwind and enjoy good company and drinks in a welcoming setting.
