About

The Irish Times Pub in Antwerp was a lively and cozy spot known for its warm Irish hospitality and bustling atmosphere. With a fashionable yet casual ambiance, it became a favorite meeting place for both tourists and students looking for a fun night out. The pub offered an array of delicious drinks, including various beers and expertly mixed cocktails, as well as Irish cuisine that perfectly complemented the lively surroundings.

Guests could enjoy free Wi-Fi, live music, and engaging activities like darts and billiards. Whether catching a sports game or enjoying some live performances, there was always something exciting happening at The Irish Times. Known for its affordable prices, patrons could enjoy a great night out for just 10 to 20 EUR per person. The great service enhanced the experience, leaving visitors with fond memories despite its closure.
