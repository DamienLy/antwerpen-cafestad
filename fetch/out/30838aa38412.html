About

Hopper is a vibrant spot in Antwerp that effortlessly blends the charm of a European café with the excitement of a local bar. Perfect for a leisurely lunch or a lively meet-up with friends, it offers a diverse selection of Belgian and European dishes, accompanied by an impressive range of beverages including beers, cocktails, and wines-all exceptionally affordable. The cozy and casual atmosphere is ideal for enjoying live music or catching a sports game, all while staying connected with free Wi-Fi. With options for outdoor seating and dine-in, it's a popular hangout for both tourists and locals looking for a fashionably relaxed setting without breaking the bank.
