About

Tucked away in Borsbeek, Antwerp, Bistro De Valk pairs a rustic ambiance with a cozy atmosphere, making it a charming spot for a leisurely meal. Known for its moderate pricing, the bistro offers a delightful selection of wines and beers to complement their scrumptious desserts and savory dishes. With options for dine-in and takeaway, guests can indulge indoors or experience the quaint, rustic allure of outdoor seating. Whether you're savoring a morning coffee or enjoying a fine dinner, Bistro De Valk offers a warm welcome and a delightful culinary experience.
