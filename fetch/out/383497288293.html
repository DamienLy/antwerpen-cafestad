About

Tucked away in the vibrant Borgerhout district of Antwerp, Foxy is the perfect setting for a casual meet-up with friends. This cozy nightlife spot combines style and comfort, offering a range of beverages from classic cocktails to a well-curated selection of wines and beers. For the hungry, Foxy's menu boasts delectable options like their famous spaghetti and a variety of classic snacks.

With a chic yet laid-back atmosphere, Foxy provides free Wi-Fi and boasts both dine-in and outdoor seating options, making it a versatile venue for any occasion. Guests consistently praise the friendly service and delightful food. Easy on the pocket, with prices ranging from 10 to 20 EUR per person, Foxy promises an enjoyable and affordable experience, perfect for unwinding after a long day or catching up with good company.
