About

De Zwaan in Antwerp provides a cozy and casual environment perfect for meeting up with friends. Known for its affordable yet high-quality offerings, this cafe has earned a commendable 4.6 on Google. The atmosphere is fashionably cozy, with a limited seating arrangement-an early arrival might secure your spot. It stands out with its prime location that appeals especially to students.

The menu is a celebration of Belgian culinary traditions, featuring small plates ideal for sharing and an impressive selection of beverages. Enjoy a varied beer selection, or opt for a warm coffee or tea. For those inclined, a choice of cocktails, liqueurs, and wine is also available.

With both indoor and outdoor seating options, De Zwaan caters to a range of preferences, including takeaway services and the convenience of making reservations. The friendly, knowledgeable staff ensures a pleasant experience, whether you're there for a quick pint while watching sports or a leisurely catch-up session. Despite its reasonable prices, the quality of service and food remains top-notch, making it a popular choice in the vibrant city of Antwerp.
