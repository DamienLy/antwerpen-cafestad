About

Lucy Chang in Antwerp is a casual hotspot perfect for a laid-back meal, whether you're with family, friends, or enjoying some solo time. Known for its fashionable yet cozy atmosphere, this popular spot serves a wide range of Asian cuisines, from Vietnamese to Laotian, catering to varied tastes and dietary preferences including vegan and vegetarian options. The menu boasts authentic recipes bursting with bold flavors, featuring specialty dishes like Pad Thai and matcha-infused delights. With a price range between 20-30 EUR per person, it's an affordable choice without compromising quality. Guests can enjoy quick service and free Wi-Fi, making it a go-to for both tourists and students. The vibrant setting, inclusive environment, and diverse drink offerings, including cocktails and a fine selection of beers, ensure a delightful dining experience. Whether dining in, grabbing takeaway, or enjoying a late meal outdoors, Lucy Chang is a must-visit for anyone craving a taste of Asia in the heart of Antwerp.
