About

Café Moeskop in Antwerp exudes a fashionably casual and cozy vibe, perfect for family dinners or catching up with friends. Known for its budget-friendly menu, with prices ranging from 1 to 10 EUR per person, it offers a variety of teas, coffees, and an impressive selection of beers and wines. Patrons can enjoy the chic atmosphere indoors or opt for the gezellig terrace with outdoor seating. Whether working remotely with free Wi-Fi or just enjoying a casual evening, this café blends charm with practicality, making it a favored local spot.
