About

Zeppelin 13 Bvba in Antwerp offers a delightful escape from the ordinary with its cozy, living room vibe. This lively spot, popular among locals, perfectly balances fashion and casual comfort. It's an ideal place for meeting friends over a delightful Belgian or European dinner without breaking the bank. You can enjoy an impressive selection of beers or savor expertly crafted cocktails and wines, all for an affordable price.

The menu features comforting dishes like dumplings and pasta, along with stimulating conversations fueled by robust coffee or a chilled beer. Zeppelin 13 Bvba hosts live music and performances, enhancing its vibrant atmosphere. Its welcoming staff contributes to a genuinely pleasant experience, whether you choose to dine indoors or enjoy the vibrant neighborhood from outdoor seating.

If you value a relaxed yet fashionable setting, excellent service, and a reasonable price range of 10 to 20 EUR per person, Zeppelin 13 Bvba is the place to be for food, drinks, and friendly company.
