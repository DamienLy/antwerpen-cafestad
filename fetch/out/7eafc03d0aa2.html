About

Bakkerij Roscam Bvba in Ekeren, Antwerp is a delightful spot where quality meets affordability. Known for its delectable chocolate treats, this bakery-cafe offers a variety of mouth-watering selections to satisfy your sweet tooth. With a Google rating of 4.5, patrons consistently praise the "supergoede bakkerij" for its delicious offerings. Perfect for a budget-friendly indulgence, you can enjoy the tasty delights with takeaway service, all for a modest cost between 10 to 20 EUR per person.
