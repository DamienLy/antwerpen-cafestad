Antwerp and Hasselt

Two Irish pubs, the big matches and live music every weekend

We have poured pints on the Grote Markt in Antwerp since 1996 and on the Fruitmarkt in Hasselt since 1997. Come in for the match, stay for the band.

The Irish Times Pub brought a traditional Irish public house to Antwerp in the summer of 1996: big screens for the international sport, live music from Ireland and further afield on Friday and Saturday nights, and staff who know the town. Hasselt followed a year later.

30 years on, the idea has not changed. A good pint, a seat for the match, a band at the weekend and a welcome whether you live here or are only passing through.
