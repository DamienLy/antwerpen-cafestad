About

Jones & Co in Antwerp is a hidden gem for those who appreciate a cozy and friendly atmosphere. Established in 2014, this cocktail bar has earned its stripes with a fantastic range of cocktails and an excellent service reputation. Whether you're settling down to enjoy the latest sports event or simply catching up with friends, the welcoming staff, who are both friendly and knowledgeable about their offerings, will make your visit enjoyable. With options for outdoor seating, dine-in, and the ability to make reservations, Jones & Co caters to all your needs. Despite its high rating of 4.7, it's surprisingly affordable, offering a diverse selection of drinks and food at a reasonable price. An inclusive venue, it warmly welcomes the LGBTQ community, ensuring every guest feels at home.
