About

Café Corso in Antwerp is a cozy spot that perfectly blends a casual atmosphere with friendly service. Rated 4.3 on Google, this café is ideal for catching up with friends over a couple of beers or sipping on a cocktail. The staff are known for their warmth and quick service, making your visit smooth and enjoyable. With an inviting outdoor seating area, it's a great place to enjoy the lively vibe of Antwerp without breaking the bank. Dishes and beverages here are affordable, with most under 10 EUR, making it perfect for those on a budget. Whether you're there to catch a game or just unwind with a coffee, Café Corso promises a relaxed and friendly experience.
