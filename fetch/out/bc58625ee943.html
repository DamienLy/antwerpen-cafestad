About

De Varkenspoot in Antwerp offers an ideal spot for a laid-back evening with friends, combining a fashionable yet cozy atmosphere. Known for its affordability, patrons can enjoy a variety of drinks including cocktails, beers, and wines without breaking the bank. The venue also serves delicious food options, making it a versatile choice for a night out. Whether you prefer outdoor seating to soak in the lively city vibe or the inviting indoor setting, De Varkenspoot accommodates all preferences. With free Wi-Fi and a reputation for being popular among tourists, it's a vibrant hangout that promises a fun and casual night.
