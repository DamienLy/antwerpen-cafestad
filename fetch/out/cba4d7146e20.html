About

Café Welkom, situated in the vibrant city of Antwerp, stands as a beacon for those seeking a casual yet cozy spot to unwind. With its affordable price range and an impressive Google rating of 4.6, this lively venue caters excellently to friends gathering for a laid-back evening. The air is filled with the enticing scent of cheese dishes, complemented perfectly by a fine selection of beers, wines, and expertly crafted cocktails. Outdoor seating presents the chance to enjoy Antwerp's charm, while convenient parking right at the door ensures a hassle-free visit. Whether renting the entire café for a special event or just dropping by for a quick drink, the friendly ambiance at Café Welkom makes it a must-visit spot for anyone looking to enjoy an enjoyable night out.
