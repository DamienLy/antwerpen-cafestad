LIVE MUSIC

200+ electrifying shows a year. From blues legends to rising stars, our stage has seen it all. For three decades, we've been at the beating heart of Antwerp's live music scene, hosting everyone from Joe Cocker to Iron Maiden. When international acts hit Belgium, they know where to find their late-night sanctuary. Find out who plays when here.

Weekly Rituals

Every Wednesday, our legendary Jam Sessions transform the stage into a melting pot of talent. Catch tomorrow's headliners sharing the spotlight with seasoned pros. And when Saturday hits, Discobar De Rockmachien cranks out 60 years of rock history, guaranteeing you'll hear those tracks that make your soul shake.

Drop by, rock out, become family. Your next favorite night out is waiting at Kid's.
