About

Den Tweede Zit is a delightful spot in Antwerp perfect for a laid-back lunch with friends. This cozy café offers a casual atmosphere where you can enjoy a range of comfort foods like cheese and omelettes, all at a reasonable price. Kick back with a hot cup of tea or savor something stronger from their selection of beer, wine, or coffee. Despite the straightforward charm, the service leaves room for improvement, adding a bit of unexpected drama to your visit. Appreciate the opportunity to dine al fresco with their outdoor seating, making it an ideal retreat after a busy day. Whether you're stopping by for a quick bite or a leisurely chat over drinks, Den Tweede Zit provides a unique ambience tailored for relaxed social gatherings.
